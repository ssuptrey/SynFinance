    # Customer Discovery Plan - SynFinance

## Mission: Find 5 people who will pay for synthetic financial data

### Target Customer Segments

#### Segment 1: FinTech Startups (Testing & Development)
**Why they need it:**
- Need realistic data to test their apps
- Can't use real customer data in dev/staging environments
- QA teams need edge cases for testing

**Company examples:**
- Razorpay (payment processing - needs test transactions)
- CRED (credit scoring - needs diverse credit profiles)  
- Jupiter, Fi Money (neobanks - need customer profiles)
- Paytm, PhonePe (need transaction patterns)
- Zerodha, Groww (need trading data for testing)

**Contact strategy:**
- Find CTO/VP Engineering on LinkedIn
- Find QA/Testing leads
- Look for "Head of Product"

**Message template:**
```
Subject: Testing data for [their product] development?

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] is building [their product]. 

Quick question: When your QA team tests transaction flows, 
what data do you use? Real data? Manually created? Something else?

I'm building a synthetic financial data generator that creates 
realistic transactions, customer profiles, and fraud patterns 
for testing environments.

Worth a 10-minute call to see if it's useful for your team?
```

## Week 2: Customer Conversations

### The 5 Questions to Ask on Every Call

1. **"How do you currently handle test data for development?"**
   - Listen for pain points
   - Note: manual work, using production data (risky), nothing

2. **"What would ideal test data look like for your use case?"**
   - This tells you what to build
   - Real requirements, not your assumptions

3. **"Have you tried any solutions for this? What worked/didn't work?"**
   - Understand alternatives
   - Learn from competitors

4. **"If I built exactly what you described, what would you pay for it?"**
   - Get real pricing expectations
   - Gauge urgency

5. **"If you had this today, when would you start using it?"**
   - "Immediately" = real pain
   - "Maybe in 6 months" = nice-to-have

### Goal for Week 2
- [ ] 5 customer calls completed
- [ ] At least 2 people say "I'd pay for this"
- [ ] Clear understanding of what features matter most

## After 2 Weeks: Decision Point

### Outcome A: 2+ people show real interest
✅ **You have product-market fit signals**
- Build an MVP focused on their exact needs
- Get them as beta customers
- Price it and close the first sale

### Outcome B: People interested but won't pay
⚠️ **You have a "nice-to-have" not "must-have"**
- Ask what would make it must-have
- Consider pivoting to adjacent problem
- Or make it open source for exposure

### Outcome C: No interest from anyone
❌ **Market validation failed**
- Don't build more features
- Either find different customer segment
- Or pivot to different problem entirely
